The path

From first flight to licence.

Nobody on the islands tells you how this works. We do. Six steps, in order. You decide how far you go.

Ask where to start

  1. Introduction flight

    Sit in the right seat. Twenty minutes over the reef with one of our pilots. Share a flight with friends to keep it affordable.

    Any age, book by WhatsApp

  2. Info session

    Three hours at Jet Center Curaçao. When to start, where to study, what it costs. Bring your parents. You ask, we answer.

    A few times a year

  3. Ground school

    Evening classes with our members. Weather, navigation, radio, rules. We also visit the tower and the airlines.

    Starts after the info session

  4. Fly with us

    Lessons in our own Cessna 152 and 172s, US-registered. Your first solo is a day you never forget.

    Solo from 16, licence from 17

  5. Study abroad, or stay

    Airline route: we connect you with schools in the USA and Europe we know. Recreational route: keep flying here with the club.

    Your call

  6. Fly home

    Our alumni fly for EZ Air and Divi Divi. Island kids in the left seat. The next one can be you.

    The goal

Our story
"We chose not to become a flight school. We keep it simple and affordable. Our main goal is to guide you."
Franklin Antoin, founder. Born and raised on Bonaire, airline captain, flight instructor.
  1. 2007

    Three students in Bonaire. Franklin, flying for DAE, starts evening classes for his brother Danielson, Amir and Nelgenn. All three go on to Florida.

  2. 2011

    Our first plane. A Cessna 152, N939AC, bought in Naples and financed by a local bank.

  3. 2013

    Falki Aviation Curaçao Foundation. A legal home for the group, now 25 members strong.

  4. 2014

    100 members registered across both islands. The peak year.

  5. 2015

    Falki Aviation Bonaire Foundation. Two foundations, the same goal.

  6. 2022

    Career seminar on both islands with Ocala Aviation from Florida. Several alumni now fly for EZ Air and Divi Divi.

Questions

Things people ask us.

How old do I have to be?
Introduction flights and the Kids Program have no real minimum. On US-registered aircraft like ours you can fly solo at 16 and hold a private pilot licence at 17. Ground school can start earlier.
What does it cost?
It depends on the route you choose, so we give a cost indication at the info session instead of a number on a website. Because we are a foundation and not a school, flying with the club stays as affordable as we can make it.
Do I need to be good at maths or physics?
You need to be curious and show up. The theory is learnable. Our members teach it in the evenings and we have seen every kind of student get through.
Do I need a medical?
Yes, before you fly solo. We tell you which class you need for your route and where on the islands to get it. Glasses are fine.
Where does the flying happen?
Curaçao from Hato, Bonaire from Flamingo. Our aircraft move between the islands and members fly to Aruba too.
I want to fly for an airline. Can you get me there?
We cannot hand you a job. We can show you the whole path, connect you with schools in the USA and Europe we have worked with, and put you in a room with pilots who did exactly this. Several of them fly for EZ Air and Divi Divi today.
